# Editorial reading layout and chart availability

The user approved restoring the earlier editorial website's hierarchy, whitespace
and responsive grouping while retaining the newly unified report content. Shared
content, typography, colors and chart geometry remain the contract; web and image
layouts serve different reading surfaces.

## Approach

Keep the shared ReportDocument adapters. Restore a primary index strip with price
and return together, a compact six-dimension score grid, paired secondary figures
on wide screens, and readable prose sections. On phones, paired figures stack and
metric strips become two columns. Weekly keeps its existing summary strip and
four trend panels; prose gains a clear heading/text relationship. Do not restore
the old duplicate metric cards or raw Markdown body. Do not flatten every panel
into one full-width list. Image exports retain their established linear layout
and shared theme; their inputs and publisher identities remain unchanged.

The latest Asia edition has no supplemental charts JSON. Its daily Markdown still
contains chartable market data. Distinguish an absent supplemental bundle from
missing individual facts, show one accurate expandable supplemental-data status,
retain published degradation reasons, and never invent observations or borrow a
different date. A parent whose rows move to history charts must not remain empty.

## Acceptance

- Every admitted panel/row remains reachable once in native reading; combined
  close/return rows and SVG export content are preserved.
- Desktop has visibly differentiated primary metrics, paired figures and prose;
  phone, both languages, light/dark and no-JavaScript remain usable.
- Five synthetic missing cards do not imply the daily metrics are absent. Actual
  missing facts and published source limitations remain available and explicit.
- Complete repository checks, visual inspection and actual publisher SVG-to-PNG
  validation precede PR, merge, Pages deployment and live verification.
